Privacy Policy for Termbook: Student Planner

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Developer: SOFTFIRE (Nichita Dolinschi), Republic of Moldova
Contact: softfire.md@gmail.com
App: Termbook: Student Planner (Android, package md.softfire.studentplanner)

Termbook is a student planner. In short: the information you enter stays on your device, and we (the developer) never receive it. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

Your terms, subjects, timetable, tasks, exams, reminders, attendance records and grades are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. There is no Termbook account, no sign-in and no Termbook server. We cannot see, access or recover this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from your device, apart from any backup you made yourself (see below).

Android's own device backup may include app data if you have enabled it in your phone's settings; that backup is managed by Google under your Google Account.

2. Backup files and optional Google Drive backup

You can export a backup file and import it again using Android's system file picker. That file is saved where you choose and is under your control.

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app saves backup files of your planner data to your own Google Drive, in a hidden app-data folder that only Termbook can access (Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

  • Drive backup is off until you enable it, and Google shows a consent screen first.
  • Backups are sent directly from your device to Google over an encrypted connection. They never pass through any server of ours.
  • You can disconnect in the app's Settings and revoke access in your Google Account (Security > Third-party connections). You can delete the backup in Google Drive (Settings > Manage apps > Termbook > Delete hidden app data).

Termbook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Termbook Pro is sold through Google Play as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Payments are processed by Google; we never receive your name, card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases are active for your Google account and stores that status on your device so Pro keeps working offline. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version may show a banner ad provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). Termbook Pro users do not see ads, and the ads SDK is not started for them.

  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches and taps), and diagnostic information (such as performance and crash data). This is used to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google advertising.
  • Because Termbook is used by teenagers, all ad requests are marked for teen treatment: personalized ads and remarketing are turned off, and ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders you set for tasks, exams and classes. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Run at startup: only to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet access: only for Google Drive backup (if enabled), Google Play purchases and ads in the free version.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Termbook Pro.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos or other files beyond a file you pick yourself.

6. No analytics or tracking by us

Termbook contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of its own. We do not sell personal data. The only third parties involved are the Google services described above.

7. Children

Termbook is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through the app, contact us.

8. Data retention, deletion and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. You can delete your data at any time in the app, by clearing app data or uninstalling, and by deleting the Drive backup as described above. Data handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and your Google Account controls. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Moldova's Law No. 133/2011 on personal data protection), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data; contact us with any request or question.

9. Security

Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Backups to Google Drive and communication with Google services use encrypted connections (TLS). No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your device locked.

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
